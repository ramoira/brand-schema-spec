# Migrating from 3.1.0 to 3.2.0

3.2.0 lets a rule say what it protects, adds governed terms and pillar ids, and records who affirmed what. Every new field is optional.

## Do I need to change anything?

No. 3.0.0 and 3.1.0 documents stay valid under the 3.2.0 validator, as long as they keep the `spec_version` they declare. A document that declares an earlier version and carries a 3.2.0 field is refused, so a consumer that only understands the earlier version is never handed a document it cannot read.

## Does it need re-ratifying?

- Changing `spec_version` to `"3.2.0"` alone: **no**. `spec_version` is outside `content_hash`.
- Adding `protects`, `term_refs`, a `pillar_id` or `voice.terms`: **yes, like any change of meaning.** These sit inside `content_hash`, so the hash changes and the ratified version stays as it was until the new one is ratified.
- Adding `draft_provenance.affirmations`: **no**. It is process metadata, outside the hash.

## What is new

| Field | In `content_hash` | What it records |
|---|---|---|
| `rules[].protects` | Yes | What the rule exists to protect: a `claim_id`, `pillar_id` or `term_id`, or a JSON pointer to a fact or principle (SPEC.md section 6.5). At least one entry when present. |
| `rules[].rubric.term_refs` | Yes | The governed terms a judged rule's question depends on |
| `narrative.pillars[].pillar_id` | Yes | A stable identifier, so a rule can say it protects the pillar |
| `voice.terms[]` | Yes | Governed terms: `{term_id, term, definition, scope_note?, example_refs?}` (SPEC.md section 8.7) |
| `draft_provenance.affirmations[]` | No | Who affirmed a rule or a field of brand meaning, and when |

None of these is a result, and no check reads `protects` or `term_refs`: they explain why a rule exists and what its words mean.

## Moving a document to 3.2.0

1. Set `ramoira.spec_version` to `"3.2.0"`.
2. Optionally give pillars a `pillar_id`, add governed terms, and say what each rule protects. Recompute the hash (`npm run hash -- brand.schema.json --write`).
3. Regenerate the summary (`npm run summarize -- brand.schema.json brand.schema.summary.json`). A public rule's `protects` entries that point into material the summary excludes (`commercial`, or `sacredBoundary` without opt-in) are dropped from the summary; the rule stays.

## Validation

- Invariant 2 also covers `pillar_id`, `term_id`, `term_refs` and terms' `example_refs`; claim, pillar and term ids share one namespace.
- Invariant 5: in a ratified schema, every example a governed term cites is judged by `brand_owner` or `brand_team`.
- Invariant 13: what a rule protects is a fact or principle.
- Invariant 14: affirmations are well-formed authority claims (a `brand_owner` or `brand_team` participant, an existing rule or a field of brand meaning).
