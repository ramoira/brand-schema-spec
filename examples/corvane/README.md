# Corvane — example schema (fictional)

Corvane is a fictional maker of carbon-steel and cast-iron pans, invented for this example. The competitor names in its rules are fictional too.

- `brand.schema.json`: the full schema. `workflow_state: in_review`, `ratification: null`. A candidate.
- `brand.schema.summary.json`: generated by `npm run summarize`. Private rules (pricing, competitors, the safety-notice rule), the commercial and governance layers and `sacredBoundary` are left out, because Corvane has not opted them in.

What to look at:

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `rules` | `deterministic_exact` (fear vocabulary, warranty wording, competitor names), `deterministic_structural` (approved claims only, discount cap, self-reference, no exclamation marks), `judged_bounded` (not a gadget, no invented heritage, social formality floor) |
| `r_no_invented_heritage` | A judged rule grounded both in two brand-judged examples and in a rail with an example and an anti-example |
| `r_safety_notice_no_promotion` | A rule that applies only while the brand has the `sit_safety_notice` situation active |
| `r_forbidden_colour`, `r_audio_tempo` | Visual and audio rules. A text-only verifier reports them `not_evaluable`. |
| `r_no_disposable_language` | A rule inherited from the anchor archetype and affirmed by the owner |
| `governance.surfaces` | Packaging copy suspends the contextual self-reference rule |
| `draft_provenance` | Who took part, the anchor, two delta answers, field statuses (one `unfilled`), and an owner reaction promoted to the rejected example `ex_pdp_gadget` |
